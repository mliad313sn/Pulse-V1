# PULSE in 3 minutes — for the busy site IT manager

**Sign in** with your email. First time: you'll be asked to set your own password (min 10 characters). That's it — no manual needed.

## Your two home pages

- **◎ Sites → your site** — everything about your site on one page: project cards, milestones due, readiness checklists, roadblocks, actions owned by your team, upcoming GO_LIVEs. Works on your phone.
- **☑ My Actions** — your open actions, roadblocks, and milestones, sorted by due date. **Projects you manage appear as cards on top.** One tap on a checkbox = done.

## The 20-second status update

Open your project → **Updates & Decisions** tab → pick a mood (ON TRACK / WATCH / AT RISK), type one sentence, press **Enter**. Done.

Do this at least every 3 weeks — a project nobody updates goes **amber at 21 days** and **red at 30 days of total silence**. The dashboard never lies on your behalf.

## Blocked? Say so — loudly

Project → **Roadblocks** tab → type the title, pick severity, Enter. If leadership needs to act, hit **Escalate ↑** — the Group IT Manager and the engaged Division Leads are notified instantly.

## If you're the Project Manager

Being PM (even as a Contributor) gives you **full control of that project**: edit its details, add milestones, mark them done (progress bars compute themselves — never type a %), close roadblocks, log decisions, write the executive commentary, and export the project deck. Everything else on the platform stays read-mostly for you — as it should.

## Need slides?

Any view → **⬇ Export deck**. Portfolio filters carry into the deck — filter to your site first and you get a site deck titled for your site. It's in Endeavour style and presentable as-is: don't touch it, just present it.

## Reading a project card in 5 seconds

- **Dot** = RAG (hover it: 4 signals — schedule, roadblocks, actions, freshness). "MANUAL" badge = someone overrode it, reason on hover.
- **Bar** = % of milestones done (computed).
- **⚑ line** = worst open roadblock. **◈ line** = next milestone and date.

All times are GMT. Everything you do is visible to everyone — that's the point.
